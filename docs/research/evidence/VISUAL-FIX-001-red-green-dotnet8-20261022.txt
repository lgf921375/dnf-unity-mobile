deactivate it in the first Update. This was a possible render-loss based on

P1-A — MINIMAL FIX / GREEN CONTRACT
CombatImpactLifetime.Begin(target, duration) now arms a per-target
first-render-opportunity flag; at the next Unity Update,
AdvanceRenderFrame(Time.unscaledDeltaTime) clears the flag WITHOUT decrementing
its requested duration, even at .25s; subsequent Update calls consume dt
normally. Repeated FixedUpdate/Consume calls before one Update cannot
prematurely reduce the new FX. Reset clears everything; each of the two
target renderers and lifetimes are independent. Existing per-target routing,
only-HP-owner and fixed60Hz clocks remain unchanged.
The pure state machine protects a render FRAME OPPORTUNITY, not an asserted
actual GPU render frame. The same-target second hit replaces the first
renderer; a future queued FX system could avoid same-target overwrites.
